# Sampling Plan

## Design

Use purposive site selection with stratified participant recruitment to reflect Ghana's digital diversity. Select locations that include an urban setting such as Greater Accra or Kumasi, a peri-urban municipality, a rural community in a northern, middle, or coastal belt, and a community with limited connectivity.

## Sample size

Target approximately 300-600 participants across sites, subject to a documented power calculation, resources, expected attrition, and ethics approval. Allocate participants between intervention and comparison conditions as evenly as practical.

## Participant groups

Recruit across secondary and tertiary students, teachers, small-business owners and informal-sector workers, public servants, market traders, parents and caregivers, older adults, persons with disabilities, and rural or local-language-speaking residents.

## Stratification variables

Track age, gender, education, location, disability accommodation needs, income proxy, device ownership or sharing, connectivity, data affordability, and language preference. These variables support equitable recruitment and subgroup analysis.

## Recruitment

Work through trusted local institutions such as schools, libraries, faith communities, youth centres, market associations, disability organisations, and district assemblies. Use accessible, plain-language recruitment materials. Avoid recruitment that pressures students, employees, or beneficiaries to participate.

## Assignment

Where random assignment is feasible and approved, assign participants or comparable groups to intervention and comparison conditions. Where it is not feasible, document the allocation process and measure baseline differences for adjustment in analysis. A delayed intervention may be used for the comparison group.

## Inclusion and accommodation

Provide local-language support, accessible venues, alternative formats, device-sharing arrangements where appropriate, and low-data or offline materials. Record accommodations and delivery constraints so that results are interpreted fairly.

## Attrition

Record attendance, reasons for dropout where voluntarily offered, and whether a participant completes baseline and endline measures. Report attrition by condition and demographic subgroup.
