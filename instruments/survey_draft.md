# Survey Draft

## Use

Administer at baseline and endline. Adapt language, examples, and response options through local pilot testing. Do not collect direct identifiers unless they are essential to approved study administration.

## A. Consent

Confirm that the participant has received an explanation of the study, understands that participation is voluntary, and agrees to take part.

## B. Background and access

1. Age group: under 18 / 18-24 / 25-34 / 35-49 / 50-64 / 65+ / prefer not to say.
2. Gender: self-describe / prefer not to say.
3. Community and setting: urban / peri-urban / rural / low-connectivity.
4. Highest education completed.
5. Preferred language for learning and digital support.
6. Do you use a smartphone, shared phone, computer, or none of these?
7. How reliable are electricity and internet access where you live? Very unreliable to very reliable.
8. How affordable is mobile data for you? Very unaffordable to very affordable.
9. Do you identify as a person with a disability or require an accessibility accommodation? Optional.

## C. AI awareness and use

10. Before today, had you heard of artificial intelligence or AI?
11. Which AI-enabled tools or services have you used? Examples: chatbot, voice assistant, translation, recommendations, customer-service bot, image generator. Select all that apply.
12. How often do you use an AI-enabled tool? Never / less than monthly / monthly / weekly / daily.
13. What do you mainly use it for? Learning, work, business, translation, public services, entertainment, communication, other.
14. How confident are you using an AI tool? Not at all confident to very confident.

## D. Functional skills and critical AI literacy

For each statement, select strongly disagree, disagree, neither agree nor disagree, agree, or strongly agree.

15. I can find an official website for a public service.
16. I can judge whether a website or message is likely to be legitimate.
17. I know that an AI answer can be inaccurate even when it sounds confident.
18. I know how to verify an AI answer using another credible source.
19. I consider whether an AI tool may be unfair to some groups.
20. I know when to seek a human opinion instead of relying on an AI response.

## E. Privacy, cybersecurity, and synthetic media

21. I would not enter a password, one-time PIN, Ghana Card number, or bank details into a public AI tool.
22. I can identify common signs of phishing or a scam message.
23. I know that AI can generate realistic images, voices, and videos that may be false.
24. I check a sender, link, and trusted source before acting on an urgent digital message.
25. I understand that uploading another person's photo, voice, or data to an AI system can raise consent and privacy concerns.

## F. Empowerment and outcomes

26. Digital tools help me complete important tasks independently.
27. I feel able to use online services more safely than before.
28. I feel able to question an automated recommendation or decision.
29. What is the most useful way AI could support you?
30. What is your biggest concern about AI or digital services?

## Administration notes

Use interviewer assistance for participants who prefer it. Read questions neutrally, record non-response, and never request account credentials or financial details.
