# Practical Skills Assessment

## Purpose

Assess observable technology-literacy skills rather than relying only on self-reported confidence. Use equivalent, not identical, baseline and endline scenarios. Do not use real credentials, money transfers, or personal data.

## Scoring

Score each task from 0 to 2: 0 = unable or unsafe response; 1 = partial completion or needs substantial assistance; 2 = completes independently and explains a safe or critical judgement. Total score: 0-12.

## Tasks

### 1. Find an official public-service source

Ask the participant to find information about a public service using a search engine or provided links. Score whether the participant identifies an official source and explains at least one reason it appears official.

### 2. Evaluate an AI explanation

Provide a short AI-generated answer to a practical question that contains at least two plausible weaknesses. Ask the participant to identify what should be checked and to name a credible independent source.

### 3. Verify a claim

Provide an AI-generated or social-media claim. Ask the participant to cross-check it using a credible independent source and state whether the claim is supported, unsupported, or uncertain.

### 4. Recognise phishing

Show a simulated phishing message containing pressure, a suspicious link, and a request for sensitive information. Ask what the participant would do. A safe response includes not clicking, not sharing credentials, and verifying through an official channel.

### 5. Protect sensitive information

Present examples of information a person might paste into a public AI tool. Ask which should not be shared: passwords, one-time PINs, Ghana Card numbers, bank details, health details, or another person's data without consent.

### 6. Respond to synthetic media

Show a clearly labelled simulated image, audio, or video claim. Ask for steps to verify it. A strong response includes checking source, date, context, independent reporting, and trusted verification channels.

## Assessor notes

Use accessible devices and language support. Record assistance provided, technical disruptions, and accommodation needs. Do not penalise a participant for connectivity failure or a device limitation outside their control.
